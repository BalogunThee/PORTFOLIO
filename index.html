Attendance System / Leave Automation

Problem:

The attendance machine was faulty, and HR was unable to generate reliable staff attendance records. Additionally, the attendance system was not integrated with the enterprise HRM software, resulting in manual and time-consuming processes for leave deductions, attendance calculations, and payroll preparation.

Action:

Requested and supervised the replacement of the faulty attendance machine, configured cloud connectivity and database integration, and automated attendance processing by developing and implementing leave management modules within the HRM application. Configured the system to automatically deduct approved leave days from the required working days for each employee, ensuring accurate attendance calculations. Enhanced attendance reports to provide detailed summaries that could be directly exported and uploaded into the payroll system.

Result:

HR now generates accurate and detailed attendance reports instantly, with automated leave adjustments and payroll-ready outputs. The solution eliminated manual calculations, improved attendance record accuracy, reduced payroll processing time, and streamlined HR operations.
